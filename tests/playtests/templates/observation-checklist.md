# RGB Core V2 Observation Checklist

Use this checklist during or immediately after a playtest.

## Vector Activity

- [ ] Player used R to transform or pressure a source.
- [ ] Player used G to change relation, timing, range, or position.
- [ ] Player used B to preserve continuity, protect, block, or sustain.
- [ ] No vector felt irrelevant in the scenario.

## Decision Quality

- [ ] Player saw at least two plausible actions.
- [ ] Best action was not obvious every round.
- [ ] Success and failure reasons were clear.
- [ ] Consequences changed the next decision.

## Encounter Objective

- [ ] Objective was understood.
- [ ] Objective could be completed without defeating every opponent.
- [ ] Pressure escalated or changed during play.
- [ ] The scene ended with a clear state change.

## Rules

- [ ] No undefined core procedure was required.
- [ ] Damage, defense, and states were resolved without improvisation.
- [ ] Any improvised ruling is recorded in the session report.
